With C and F as landmarks, the other five white keys fall into place. The white keys run through the alphabet from left to right, starting again after G.

Play up the white keys below, saying each name.

```widget
type: keyboard
range: [C4, C5]
labels: naturals
```

## Around the two black keys

The group of two black keys has three white keys around it:

- **C** on the left
- **D** in the middle, between the two black keys
- **E** on the right

## Around the three black keys

The group of three has four white keys around it:

- **F** on the left
- **G** and **A** in the middle, between the black keys
- **B** on the right

After B comes the next C, and the pattern begins again.

> C D E sit around the two black keys. F G A B sit around the three.

Try to know each key by its position, not by counting up from C every time. D is easy: it is the only white key that sits exactly in the middle of the pair. G and A are the two inside the group of three, G on the left and A on the right.
