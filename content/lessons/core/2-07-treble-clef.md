In the treble clef, line 2 is G. Everything else comes from counting letters up and down from there.

## The lines

From the bottom, the five lines are **E, G, B, D, F**. Press each one.

```widget
type: staff
clef: treble
notes: [E4, G4, B4, D5, F5]
labels: true
```

A traditional memory phrase is "**E**very **G**ood **B**oy **D**oes **F**ine". Notice that lines skip a letter each time, because the letters in between live in the spaces.

## The spaces

From the bottom, the four spaces are **F, A, C, E**. They spell the word FACE.

```widget
type: staff
clef: treble
notes: [F4, A4, C5, E5]
labels: true
```

> Treble lines: E G B D F. Treble spaces: F A C E.

## Reading, not reciting

The phrases get you started, but they are slow. A faster habit is to know a few **landmarks** cold and step from the nearest one:

- **G** on line 2, where the clef curls.
- **C** in space 3.
- **F** on the top line.

Try the widget below. Press a key on the keyboard and watch where the note goes on the staff, or press a line or space on the staff and see which key it is.

```widget

```

Here is a tune that moves almost entirely by step, so you can follow it note by note. Before you play it, try naming the first four notes.

```widget

title: Ode to Joy (Beethoven, Symphony No. 9), opening

tempo: 96
```

The D and C in that tune hang below the bottom line. Notes outside the five lines are covered two lessons from now.
