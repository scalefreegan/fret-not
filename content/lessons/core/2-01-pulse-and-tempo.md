So far every note has been about *which* sound. This level is about *when*.

Almost all music has a **pulse**: a steady, evenly spaced beat running underneath it, like a ticking clock. It is what you tap your foot to. The notes of a tune can be long or short, but they are all measured against that beat.

Press start and tap your finger along with the lights.

```widget
type: metronome
tempo: 72
time: 4/4
```

The machine above is a **metronome**. Its only job is to keep a perfectly even beat.

## Tempo

**Tempo** is the speed of the beat. It is measured in **beats per minute**, written BPM. At 60 BPM there is one beat every second. At 120 BPM there are two.

Change the tempo in the metronome to 60, then to 120, and tap along with each. A slow walk is around 60 to 80 BPM. A brisk pop song is often around 120.

> Tempo changes how fast the beats arrive. It does not change which notes are played, or how long each note lasts *counted in beats*.

Here is the same tune twice. Play the first, then the second. Nothing about the notes is different: only the tempo.
