Music is made of silences as well as sounds, and the silences are measured just as exactly. A measured silence is called a **rest**.

Every note value has a rest that lasts the same time:

- A **whole rest** lasts 4 beats. It is a small block hanging *below* a line.
- A **half rest** lasts 2 beats. It is the same block sitting *on top of* a line.
- A **quarter rest** lasts 1 beat. It looks like a squiggle.
- An **eighth rest** lasts half a beat. It looks like a slanted stroke with one small flag.

Play this and listen for the gaps. The beat keeps going through a rest. You just don't play.

```widget
type: rhythm
pattern: "q qr q qr | h hr | q q qr q | wr"
tempo: 80
```

> A rest takes up time exactly like a note does. Count it, feel it, and stay silent.

Telling the whole rest from the half rest is a common stumbling block. One way to remember: the whole rest is heavier (4 beats), so it *hangs down* from its line. The half rest is lighter, so it *sits up* on its line like a hat.

Now tap this one. Tap only on the notes, and let the rests go by.

```widget
type: rhythm
pattern: "q q qr q | q qr q qr | h qr q | q q h"
tempo: 76
tap: true
```
